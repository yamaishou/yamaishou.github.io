I am an MPhil student in the **Department of Physics** at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**. 

### Research Interests
My work focuses on theoretical cosmology and quantum field theory in curved spacetime, including:
* **Inflationary Cosmology & Primordial Black Holes:** Multi-field inflation dynamics and power spectrum enhancements $P_\zeta(k)$.
* **Open Effective Field Theory & In-In Formalisms:** Schwinger-Keldysh techniques and loop corrections in de Sitter spacetime.
* **Quantum Gravity & Holography:** Unimodular gravity wavefunctions and $\text{dS}/\text{CFT}$ correspondence.
